Your own name for an address. Match an IP (160.79.104.10), a range (160.79.104.0/21), or any text that appears in a hostname or owner (anthropic). The most specific range wins.

Hidden

Rows matching these are counted but not shown, unless you click N hidden in the bar. Same match forms as aliases, and a name here matches the alias, owner or hostname a row is known by.

Excluded owners

Hide everything belonging to an organisation. Unlike a hide rule, this matches the owner only — excluding Cloudflare will not catch a host that merely has the word in its hostname. Switch one off to park it without losing it. Needs the owner toggle on to have anything to match.

Minimum rate

Hide connections moving less than this. Idle sockets stay out of the way; anything above the line is real traffic. Counted rows still appear in N hidden.
KB/s

Rate bars

The bar behind each KB/s figure. Scaled colour keeps a trickle faint and makes a megabyte a second impossible to miss; fixed colour is the old flat cyan at every speed.

Confirm before killing

Ask first, every time a process is sent a signal — the ✕ button and both right-click entries, SIGTERM and SIGKILL alike. There is no undo for a kill, and the row you clicked is one line among hundreds that reorder themselves as traffic moves. Turn it off if you would rather not be asked.
